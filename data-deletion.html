VoyTrack – Data Deletion Instructions

You may request deletion of your personal data stored by VoyTrack at any time.

How to Request Data Deletion

Send an email to:

Email: voytrack2025@gmail.com

Your message should include:

  • Your registered name
  • Your phone number
  • Your account ID (if known)

What Data Will Be Deleted?

  • Personal profile information
  • Gym fee and payment records
  • WhatsApp integration tokens
  • Usage and analytics data

How Long Does It Take?

Data deletion requests are processed within 48–72 hours.

Contact

If you have questions, reach out at: voytrack2025@gmail.com
